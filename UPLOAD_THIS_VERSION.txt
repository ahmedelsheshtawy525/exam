FMVA Exam Platform v12 — Student Results Review

This version keeps the existing exam submission/result summary and adds a full student Results view.

Student flow:
1. Submit exam normally.
2. Immediate completion page remains the score summary.
3. Click Results in the top navigation after leaving the exam.
4. The latest completed result opens as the same score-summary style page.
5. Under the summary, the student sees every question, their submitted answer, the correct answer, correctness, and points.
6. From the dashboard, clicking any row under My results opens that result's detailed review.

Backend:
- Added GET /api/results/:id for the logged-in owner only.
- Returns result metadata plus answer/question details.
- Does not expose other students' results.

Frontend cache version: app.js?v=12.0
