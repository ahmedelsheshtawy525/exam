RETAKE FIX v22.1

Root cause of the 500 on submit:
exam_attempts had UNIQUE(exam_id,user_id,status). When a student already had a
submitted attempt, a new attempt could start as in_progress, but submitting it
changed its status to submitted and collided with the old submitted row.

Fix:
Migration 0012 rebuilds exam_attempts without the three-column UNIQUE constraint.
It adds a partial unique index only for status='in_progress', so:
- multiple submitted attempts are allowed;
- multiple expired attempts are allowed;
- only one in-progress attempt exists per student/exam.

No certificate/email is generated for failed attempts. Certificate/email remains
restricted to passed attempts in worker.js.
